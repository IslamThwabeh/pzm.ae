Our Quality Process

How We Test Every Used Device

Each device goes through a 6-point check at our Al Barsha store before it is listed or handed over.

🔋 Battery Health

Battery capacity is measured and clearly listed. Devices below 80% health are flagged or serviced before sale.

🖥️ Screen & Display

We check for dead pixels, burn-in, and colour accuracy. Any screen issues are noted in the listing grade.

📷 Camera & Sensors

Front and rear cameras, Face ID, Touch ID, and proximity sensors are tested across all modes before listing.

🔌 Charging & Connectivity

Charging ports, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and mobile connectivity are verified. Loose or slow-charging ports are serviced first.

📦 Body & Condition Grade

Physical wear is assessed and graded Excellent, Good, or Fair. The grade is explained before purchase.

🔄 Factory Reset & Unlock

Every device is fully reset, iCloud and Samsung account cleared, and software cleaned before handover.

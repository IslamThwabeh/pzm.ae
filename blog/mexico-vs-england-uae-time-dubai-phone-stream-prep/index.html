Mexico 2-3 England: World Cup Result and What Came Next

Archived event guide: This event has ended. Schedule details and references to “today” below are retained only as a dated record and are not current advice.

Result confirmed: England beat Mexico 3-2 in the Round of 16 and advanced to face Norway. This page originally carried the UAE kickoff time; it now records the result and prepares viewers for England’s next early-morning match.

What happened next for England?

England beat Norway 2-1 after extra time in the quarterfinal, then lost 2-1 to Argentina in the semifinal. The final is today: Argentina face Spain at 11:00 PM GST. See the full final schedule and results in UAE time.

What to prepare before sleeping

  • Set phone and TV reminders before midnight.
  • Charge your phone, headphones, remote, and power bank.
  • Open the streaming app once and confirm the account is signed in.
  • Keep a charger beside the sofa or bed if you plan to watch on mobile.
  • Use headphones if others at home are sleeping.

Phone battery warning signs

If your iPhone loses 20-30% overnight or shuts down under 20%, do not rely on it as your only screen for a 1:00 AM match. Use a charger, power bank, or get the battery checked before finals week.

Screen and protector check

Early morning watching often means tired hands and accidental drops. If your screen protector is already cracked, replace it before the match instead of risking the main display.

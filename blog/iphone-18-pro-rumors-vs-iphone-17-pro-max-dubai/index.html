iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max: what Dubai buyers can confirm

Apple has announced iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. Earlier rumors and price estimates on this page are now superseded by Apple's official UAE specifications. PZM's selling prices and local availability are confirmed on enquiry.

What Apple confirms

Both models use the A20 Pro chip and 48MP Pro Fusion camera system. iPhone 18 Pro has a 6.3-inch display and weighs 211 g; Pro Max has a 6.9-inch display and weighs 249 g. Apple lists Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy finishes, with 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB capacities for each model.

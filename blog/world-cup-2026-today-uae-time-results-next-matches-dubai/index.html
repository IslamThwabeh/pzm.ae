Fast watch-setup checklist

For late-night and early-morning matches, check the TV app login, restart the router, test HDMI, charge the remote, and keep a fast charger and power bank near the sofa. If friends are coming over, add one spare USB-C cable and a surge-protected extension strip.

Phone and stream checks that prevent match-night problems

  • Battery health: if your iPhone drops fast below 40%, get it checked before the quarter-finals.
  • Screen: install a protector before guests, snacks, and late-night sofa drops do their thing.
  • Streaming: test from the exact room where you will watch, not beside the router.
  • Audio: connect the soundbar before kickoff so commentary and crowd noise are balanced.

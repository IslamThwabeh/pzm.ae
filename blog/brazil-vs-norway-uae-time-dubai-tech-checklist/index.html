Brazil 0-2 Norway: World Cup Result and What Came Next

Result confirmed: Norway beat Brazil 2-0 in the Round of 16, ending Brazil’s tournament and sending Norway into the quarterfinals. This page originally carried the UAE kickoff time; it has now been converted into a result and next-match guide.

What happened next for Norway?

Norway's run ended in the quarterfinals with a 2-1 extra-time loss to England. England then lost 2-1 to Argentina in the semifinal. The final is today: Argentina vs Spain at 11:00 PM GST. See the complete final schedule and results in UAE time.

Fast checklist before Argentina vs Spain

  • Charge your phone and power bank before 10:30 PM.
  • Test the streaming app and login before guests arrive.
  • Use a stable HDMI cable if you are casting from laptop or console.
  • Keep one USB-C cable and fast charger near the watching area.
  • Restart the router if your stream has been unstable this week.

For phone-heavy match nights

A 1:00 AM kickoff means your phone may already be low before the match begins. Charge it early, keep a power bank nearby, and test quiet audio if others at home are sleeping.

For TV and streaming

If the stream freezes, do not blame the TV first. Check router placement, app updates, HDMI cable quality, and whether other devices are downloading updates. For the cleanest setup, use Ethernet where possible.
